# Mizan monthly workspace

Mode: Operate. The user delegated naming, identity, architecture and design decisions with full creative autonomy; this is the authorization for the replacement visual world and the corresponding design documentation refresh.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A monthly financial workspace that makes recorded income, expenses, and the remaining allowance immediately understandable. Preserve calendar-month accounting, local data, optional cloud sync and document reconciliation.

OWN-WORLD: Ink navy navigation, iris selection and action color, ice-tinted ground, white reading surfaces, mint income and coral expense series. DM Sans Variable remains locally bundled with Turkish glyphs. A folded M is the identity, never a stock wallet icon.

STORY: See the month, inspect the spending rhythm, follow a category to its records, then add a record. Optional onboarding demonstrates the arithmetic with explicitly synthetic values before asking for setup.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Persistent navigation on desktop; month and account toolbar; monthly heading; a balance composition with income/expense columns and adjacent monthly allowance; spending chart and category distribution; recent records and card calendar below. On phones, the same hierarchy becomes a single column above a safe-area-aware bottom navigation.

FORM: Grounded candidate 3, contemporary music publication identity: clear humanist typography, violet ink on pale stock, a visible rhythm of data. Other candidates considered: metro signage, museum identity, music publication, archive labels, Turkish ceramics, calendar stationery and chromatic paper swatches. seed:d49bcd7ce3cd99e1. Catalog challengers supply no layout or decorative costume; operational clarity determines the shell. Signature move: a state-driven budget ribbon changes its filled proportion and text with real recorded spending, paired with a daily/cumulative chart toggle. Reduced motion snaps all states.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
